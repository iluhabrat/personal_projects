📌 Common Stats Interview Questions

When to use mean vs median?
Use median when data is skewed or has outliers (e.g. salaries, house prices). Use mean when data is symmetric and normally distributed.
What is a good standard deviation?
Context-dependent. A low std dev means data is clustered near the mean. Use CV = (σ/μ) × 100% to compare variability across different datasets.
How do you detect outliers?
IQR method: values below Q1 − 1.5×IQR or above Q3 + 1.5×IQR are outliers. Also use Z-score > 3 for normally distributed data.
What does skewness tell you?
Positive skew: tail on right, mean > median (e.g. income). Negative skew: tail on left, mean < median. Skewness > |1| is considered significant.
